    ive always struggled with hurting other people, specifically the people that care about me. im not 100% sure how it happens yet, i dont know if theres just something inherently wrong or evil about me, i just cant seem to be able to not hurt the ones that genuinely care about me and show me love, and in the end, i always end up hurting them and driving them away. i wish i wasnt this way, i wish i wasnt so fucking fucked in head the way i am, something something stupid fucking mental illness. i hate that i have to use this as an excuse, a pathetic and disgusting feeble excuse. im sorry dil, i promise i really meant what i said, i dont want to hurt anyone, im sorry that ive hurt you in the past and that ive hurt you again even after i thought i had changed. i always end up finding myself at the edge of pain that i so familiarly hate, stepping along side by side with the so bittersweet feeling of neglect and lethargy that has accompanied me for my entire existence, like an immovable shackle tightly wrapped around my ankle. why is it that i, who have been hurt by others' neglect reflect that back on to the people that havent hurt me? the ones that have tried to show me tenderness and love? i feel like im suffering from some sort of fuckass stockholm syndrome dealing with this shit. ive noticed this before, the eerie and throat-tightening feeling of being stuck in a loop of suffering, stuck in a cycle that repeats itself over and over, no matter how much you try to change it, at the end of it, you can't outrun your true nature.
